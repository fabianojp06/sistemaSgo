**As a** CTCEA budget analyst preparing the accountability report / the work plan of a
Partnership Agreement,
**I want** the Disbursement Schedule to be presented as a table of **periodic installments**
(T1, T2, T3...), grouped by year with subtotals and a grand total,
**So that** the document generated by SGO is identical to the APÊNDICE J that the granting body
requires — today the screen produces a month-by-month grid that does not match the official
format.

## Context and business rules

### What exists today (US-138 / UC04.01)

`montarCronogramaDesembolso.ts` generates **one row per calendar month** of the Proposal's
validity period, distributing the cost of Employee (snapshots), Trip (all in the 1st month — a
known limitation), Asset (`ItemPatrimonial`) and `RateioImpostoGrade` (in the month of its
respective date field). Columns: Monthly Disbursement, Accumulated, Accumulated Financial %
(RN0252), Amount Transferred every 12 months (RN0253). 12-month closing rows are only visually
highlighted — **there is no yearly subtotal or grand total**, and no concept of "installment" or
"Stage."

### ANEXO 9 layout (7 columns)

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| **EVENT** | `T1`, `T2`, ... `Tn` on installment rows; `YEAR XXXX` on subtotal rows; `YEARS 2026, 2027 ... AND 2030` on the grand total row. |
| **DATE** | Month spelled out + year — `"January 2026"`, `"May 2026"`, `"September 2026"`. Empty on subtotal/total rows. |
| **EVENT DESCRIPTION** | Installment: `"Nth installment relating to Stage M of the Physical Schedule"` (T1 may read `"1st installment relating to Stages 1 and 2..."` — see the Stage rule). Sub-row: `"Event Tn Goal 01"`. Subtotal: `"TOTAL TO BE DISBURSED IN XXXX"`. Grand total: `"TOTAL TO BE DISBURSED IN 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029 AND 2030"`. |
| **MONTHLY DISBURSEMENT** | Installment: the installment's amount. "Goal 01" sub-row: repeats the installment's amount (the single sub-row). Subtotal: sum of the installments of the calendar year. |
| **ACCUMULATED DISBURSEMENT** | RN0251 — progressive sum of the installments. On installment and subtotal rows. Empty on the sub-row. |
| **ACCUMULATED FINANCIAL %** | RN0252 — Accumulated ÷ Global Amount × 100, 2 decimal places, Half-Even. On installment **and** subtotal rows. |
| **AMOUNT ACCUMULATED PER YEAR OF THE PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT** | Filled **only on the `TOTAL TO BE DISBURSED IN XXXX` rows** = the Accumulated Disbursement at the end of that calendar year (the same number as the "ACCUMULATED DISBURSEMENT" column on that row). |

**Grand total** row (last one): `EVENT = "YEARS 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029 AND 2030"`,
`DESCRIPTION = "TOTAL TO BE DISBURSED IN 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029 AND 2030"`, and the Global Amount
in a merged cell. Document header: **`ANEXO 9` / `CRONOGRAMA DE DESEMBOLSO`**.

> **Out of spec:** the "Amount Transferred every 12 months of execution" column and RN0253 (the
> 12-month cycle). ANEXO 9 has no such column — instead it has "Amount Accumulated per Year of the
> Partnership Agreement," which is just the accumulated total at the end of the year.

### Business decisions closed with the user (AskUserQuestion, 2026-09-02)

| GAP | Decision |
|---|---|
| **CD-01** — transfer calendar | **Configurable per Proposal**, via 2 fields: **number of installments per year** + **initial transfer month**. E.g., "3/year starting in January" → Jan/May/Sep (spacing of 12÷3 = 4 months); "2/year starting in March" → Mar/Sep. There is no longer a "fixed Jan/May/Sep rule." |
| **CD-03** — 1st installment | There is an **entry installment (T1) in the month of `dataInicio`**. The remaining ones follow the configured calendar. If `dataInicio` coincides with the month of the 1st transfer (the ANEXO 9 case: start Jan/26, transfers starting in January), T1 accumulates both roles. |
| **CD-03b** — period covered | **Forward-looking.** Each installment Tk pays for the block that **starts** on its date: T1 (Jan) → Jan–Apr, T2 (May) → May–Aug, T3 (Sep) → Sep–Dec, ... The **last** installment covers from its date to `dataFim`. (This is why T1 is larger in ANEXO 9 — it carries Jan–Apr of operations **plus** the entire mobilization/travel/assets cost of the project, which today falls in the 1st month — see CD-06.) |
| **CD-04** — level of detail | **Single Goal.** The Partnership Agreement = 1 Goal / CONSOLIDATED Proposal. Each installment has **one** sub-row `"Event Tn Goal 01"` that repeats the installment's full amount in the MONTHLY DISBURSEMENT column. No apportionment between Goals, no special tax handling. |
| **CD-02** — Stage | **Stage 1 = the entry; each regular transfer date = the next Stage.** If `dataInicio` coincides with the 1st transfer: T1 → `"1st installment relating to Stages 1 and 2 of the Physical Schedule"` and from there on Tn (n≥2) → `"nth installment relating to Stage (n+1) of the Physical Schedule"`. If it does **not** coincide: T1 → `"1st installment relating to Stage 1..."`, T2 → `"...Stage 2..."`, with no offset (Tn → Stage n). Wording: "**Physical** Schedule" (not "Physical-Financial"). |
| **CD-05** — column 7 | It is **"AMOUNT ACCUMULATED PER YEAR OF THE PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT,"** not "Amount Transferred every 12 months." Filled only on the `TOTAL TO BE DISBURSED IN XXXX` rows = Accumulated Disbursement at the end of that **calendar year**. RN0253 and the 12-month cycle column are **out** of the spec. |
| **Advance of the 1st+2nd installment** | **Modeled as a date coincidence**, not as a cash advance. When `dataInicio` = the month of the 1st transfer, T1 is already the merge of the entry with the 1st transfer ("Stages 1 and 2") — that is what ANEXO 9 shows. The system follows the calendar's theoretical dates. |
| **Screen scope** | The current monthly grid is **REPLACED** by the installment view. There are not two screens. |

### Installment derivation rule (closed — the ADR only decides the modeling)

1. **Calculation basis:** the current month-by-month engine (`montarCronogramaDesembolso.ts`)
   remains the "source of truth" for cost by accrual period. US-142 adds an **installment
   aggregation** layer on top of it.
2. **Installment dates:**
   - Regular dates: starting from `mesInicialRepasse`, spaced `12 ÷ parcelasPorAno` months apart,
     repeating every year, keeping only those that fall within
     `[firstDayOfMonth(dataInicio), endMonth]`.
   - Entry date: the month of `dataInicio`.
   - `dates = uniqueSort([entry, ...regulars])` — if the entry is already a regular date, it
     counts **only once** (T1 gets "Stages 1 and 2").
   - Number T1, T2, ... Tn chronologically.
3. **Period covered by each installment (FORWARD-LOOKING):** from `Dk` (inclusive) to the month
   before `D(k+1)`. T1 covers `[dataInicio, D2 − 1 month]`. The last installment covers
   `[Dn, dataFim]`.
4. **Installment amount:** sum of the `desembolsoMensal` (current engine) of the months in its
   period.
5. **Sub-row per installment:** one `"Event Tn Goal 01"` row = the installment's full amount.
6. **Grand total = the Proposal's Global Amount** (RN_CD_002). Any rounding residue (cents) goes
   to the last installment.
7. **Yearly subtotals:** `YEAR XXXX / TOTAL TO BE DISBURSED IN XXXX` row at the end of each
   **calendar year** with installments — sum of the year, accumulated total, accumulated % and
   (column 7) the repeated accumulated total.
8. **Stage (description text):** the Stage counter = position in the list `[entry, regular₁,
   regular₂, ...]` (entry = Stage 1). T1 = "Stages 1 and 2" when the entry coincides with
   regular₁; otherwise "Stage 1." Tn (n≥2) = "Stage (n + offset)," offset = 1 if T1 is a merge,
   0 if not.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Scenario 1 — Schedule generation (ANEXO 9 case: start coincides with the 1st transfer)**
```gherkin
Given the Proposal has an active Version, financial data, and a Global Amount of R$ 194,981,162.00
And the validity period starts in 2026-01 (January/2026)
And parcelasPorAno = 3 and mesInicialRepasse = 1 (January) → transfers in Jan / May / Sep
When the user opens the Disbursement Schedule tab
Then the entry (Jan) coincides with the 1st transfer → T1 = January/2026 with description "1st installment relating to Stages 1 and 2 of the Physical Schedule"
And T2 = May/2026 "2nd installment relating to Stage 3 of the Physical Schedule"; T3 = September/2026 "3rd installment relating to Stage 4..."; ... ; T14 = May/2030 "14th installment relating to Stage 15..."
And each Tn has a sub-row "Event Tn Goal 01" repeating the installment's amount in the MONTHLY DISBURSEMENT column
And T1 pays for Jan–Apr/2026 (+ all mobilization/travel/assets cost, CD-06); T2 pays for May–Aug/2026; T3 pays for Sep–Dec/2026
And at the end of 2026 there is a "YEAR 2026 / TOTAL TO BE DISBURSED IN 2026" row with the year's sum, accumulated total, % and the "AMOUNT ACCUMULATED PER YEAR OF THE PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT" column = the accumulated total
And the last row is "YEARS 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029 AND 2030 / TOTAL TO BE DISBURSED IN ..." = R$ 194,981,162.00
And no field is editable [SHIELDED SOURCE / RN_CD_001]
And the operation is recorded in HistoricoOperacao
```

**Scenario 1b — Start does NOT coincide with the 1st transfer**
```gherkin
Given parcelasPorAno = 2 and mesInicialRepasse = 3 (March) → transfers in Mar / Sep
And dataInicio in 2020-05 (May)
When the schedule is generated
Then T1 = May/2020 (entry) "1st installment relating to Stage 1 of the Physical Schedule"
And T2 = September/2020 "2nd installment relating to Stage 2..."; T3 = March/2021 "3rd installment relating to Stage 3..."
And there is no Stage offset (T1 is only the entry, not a merge)
And T1 pays for May–Aug/2020; T2 pays for Sep/2020–Feb/2021; T3 pays for Mar–Aug/2021
```

**Scenario 2 — Sum of installments matches the Total Cost (RN_CD_002)**
```gherkin
Given the schedule has been generated
When the amounts of all installments Tn are summed
Then the result is exactly equal to the Proposal's Global Amount
And any rounding residue (cents) is absorbed by the last installment
```

**Scenario 3 — Accumulated Financial % (RN0252)**
```gherkin
Given installment T3 has an accumulated disbursement of R$ 8,734,745.00 and the Global Amount is R$ 36,914,000.00
When the system calculates the % of row T3
Then it displays "23.65%" (2 fixed decimal places, Half-Even rounding)
```

**Scenario 4 — Proposal with no financial data (block, Flow E1)**
```gherkin
Given the Proposal has a Global Amount of R$ 0.00 or no analytical records
When the user opens the Disbursement Schedule tab
Then no installment table is displayed
And the system displays "Operation Rejected: The selected Proposal has no financial data registered for consolidation." [ERROR LOCK]
```

**Scenario 5 — PDF/XLSX export in the ANEXO 9 layout**
```gherkin
Given the installment schedule is rendered
When the user triggers [Print]/[PDF] or [XLSX]
Then the generated file has the header "ANEXO 9 / CRONOGRAMA DE DESEMBOLSO," the 7 columns, the installments, the "Event Tn Goal 01" sub-rows, the yearly subtotals, and the grand total
And the operation is recorded in HistoricoOperacao [RN0232]
```

**Scenario 6 — Missing transfer configuration**
```gherkin
Given the Proposal does not have parcelasPorAno / mesInicialRepasse filled in
When the schedule is generated
Then the system applies the default parcelasPorAno = 3, mesInicialRepasse = 1 (January) — it does not block [ADR-049 §A]
```

**Scenario 7 — "AMOUNT ACCUMULATED PER YEAR OF THE PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT" column**
```gherkin
Given the schedule has installments in years 2026 through 2030
When the table is assembled
Then column 7 is EMPTY on every installment row and sub-row
And on the "TOTAL TO BE DISBURSED IN 2026" row, column 7 shows the Accumulated Disbursement at the end of 2026
And the same applies to every "TOTAL TO BE DISBURSED IN XXXX" row
```
